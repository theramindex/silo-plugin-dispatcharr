# Sports fallback artwork

The baseball scoreboard uses a generic baseball field photograph as decorative
fallback artwork. It does not identify the current event's venue. Provider event
artwork continues to take precedence when available.

- Photographer: Robert Bye
- Photo: https://unsplash.com/photos/baseball-field-during-night-time-0B_S1vTY0NU
- Asset: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1431817986760-7cc7fbb937b2
- License: https://unsplash.com/license (verified 8 September 2026 UTC)
- Use: cropped and shaded behind separately rendered team logos, league logo,
  team names, and scores. Served from the image CDN at 1200 px width.

GameThumbs supplies the separate team and league logos. Other sports retain
GameThumbs matchup backgrounds. An unavailable background leaves the scoreboard
and logo fallbacks intact.
